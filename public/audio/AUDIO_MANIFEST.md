### Format Requirements:
- Format: WAV (consistent with existing)
- Sample Rate: 44.1kHz or 48kHz
- Bit Depth: 16-bit or 24-bit
- Duration: 0.1s - 2.0s (short & snappy)
- Style: Playful, cartoon-like, NOT corporate/industrial
